PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Reliable measurement,
made easier at home.

PressScope 1.0 combines an intelligent cuff, guided positioning and automatic trend organization to make routine blood-pressure monitoring easier for the whole family.

CORE FUNCTIONS

Comfortable checks with fewer mistakes.

01

Fit detection

The cuff checks tightness and placement before measurement.

02

Motion guidance

Prompts identify movement that may affect a reading.

03

Irregular pulse notice

Pulse patterns are checked during each measurement.

04

Family trends

Separate profiles keep long-term records organized.

A coordinated cuff
and display system.

The preformed cuff, pressure sensor and quiet pump work with the connected display to guide positioning and provide readable results.
