PRODUCT OVERVIEW

See the cells.
Understand the details.

A desktop hematology development concept for veterinary hospitals and clinic groups. A disposable microfluidic cartridge holds the sample while brightfield and fluorescence imaging support AI analysis. Planned outputs include CBC parameters and cell images for veterinarian review. Development focuses on dogs and cats; other species require separate validation.

CORE FUNCTIONS

Connect the numbers
with the cells.

01

Five-part differential

Planned differentiation of neutrophils, lymphocytes, monocytes, eosinophils and basophils, alongside red blood cell and platelet analysis.

02

Single-use microfluidics

The sample and reagents remain within a disposable cartridge rather than a conventional wet analyzer fluid circuit. Used cartridges require appropriate biological waste handling.

03

AI-assisted morphology

Cell images are retained for review. Platelet clumping and abnormal-cell flags are planned capabilities requiring performance validation.

04

Integrated touchscreen

A 10.1-inch display combines sample operation, results and image review. The analysis time design target is 3–5 minutes, subject to validation.

DEVICE STRUCTURE

A compact system.
A complete imaging chain.

Exterior and internal views are taken from this product’s engineering concept model. The upper assembly combines touch interaction and computing, above a front-loading cartridge chamber with integrated optics, motion and control.

  1. Display and computing10.1-inch touchscreen and Windows 11 industrial computing for interaction, image processing and AI inference.
  2. Cartridge chamberFront access door, motorized tray and cartridge identification.
  3. Optics and motion5 MP camera, 20×/40× objectives, XY scanning and Z-axis autofocus.
  4. Control and coolingSTM32 controller, 24 V power, USB connectivity and a HEPA-filtered cooling path.
